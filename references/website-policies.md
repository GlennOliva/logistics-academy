## 1. Privacy Policy

Logistics VA Training Academy ("we", "us") is operated by Angela Valiente. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

### Information we collect
- Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored securely).
- Payment details: the payment method you choose, your payment reference or proof of payment, and the amount paid. We do not store full card numbers.
- Learning data: your language choice (English or Bisaya), lesson progress, knowledge check and final quiz results, and certificate records.
- Technical data: browser type, device type, and basic usage data collected through cookies.

### How we use your information
- To create and manage your student account.
- To verify your payment and give you access to the course.
- To track your progress and issue your certificate.
- To contact you about your account, payments, or course updates.
- To improve the website and courses.

### Sharing your information
We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site (such as hosting, database, email, and payment services), and only as needed for them to do so. We may also disclose it if required by law.

### Certificate verification
Each certificate has a unique ID. If someone enters that ID on our verification page, we may confirm your name, the course, and the completion date.

### Cookies
We use cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site may not work properly.

### How long we keep your data
We keep your account and completion records for as long as your account is active, and as long as needed afterward to verify certificates and meet legal or accounting requirements.

### Your rights
Under the Data Privacy Act, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, object to its processing, and withdraw your consent, subject to legal limits. Certificate records may be kept so your certificate can still be verified.

### Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No online system is completely secure, so please keep your password private.

### Age
Our courses are intended for users 18 years and older.

### Changes and contact
We may update this policy and will post the new date on this page. Questions or requests: [email address].
